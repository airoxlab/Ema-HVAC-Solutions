## Positioning

Leads with **fast AC repair** per the brief — correct for the inland Valley, which runs hotter
than the coast and well past 100 degrees in summer. The urgency section uses a 100°+ display
figure and lists the four symptoms people actually search for.

The FAQ includes a practical safety tip (switch the system off if the outdoor unit runs but the
air is warm, to avoid compressor damage) — the kind of advice that earns trust from someone
reading at 2pm in a hot house.
